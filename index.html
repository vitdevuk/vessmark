One working day

From first light
to the keys.

Four moments from an ordinary Tuesday. Different rooms, different trades, the same drawing set.

07:30

On site before the concrete

The site engineer walks the reinforcement and signs it off. Nothing is poured until the model and the steel agree.

10:00

At the drawing board

Architects and the client mark up the plans together. Every change is priced the same day.

14:00

Model review

The physical model sits next to the drawings. If something looks wrong at 1:100, it is fixed before it costs anything.

18:30

Handover at dusk

The lights go on for the first time. The client gets the keys, the warranty file and our phone number.
